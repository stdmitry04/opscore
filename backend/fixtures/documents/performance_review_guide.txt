PERFORMANCE REVIEW GUIDE — Acme Corporation HR
Cycle: Semi-annual (June and December)

OVERVIEW
Performance reviews at Acme are designed to recognize contributions, identify growth areas, and align compensation with impact. We use a calibrated rating system to ensure fairness across teams. Reviews consist of self-assessments, manager assessments, and peer feedback. The output of each review cycle is a performance rating, development plan, and compensation recommendation.

RATING SCALE
Exceptional (E): Consistently exceeds expectations. Delivers outsized impact beyond the scope of their role. Sets the standard for others. Expected frequency: top 10% of employees.
Strong (S): Meets and frequently exceeds expectations. Reliable, high-quality output. Positive impact on team culture. Expected frequency: top 30-40% of employees.
Meets Expectations (ME): Consistently meets role expectations. Delivers required work at expected quality. May have some areas for improvement. Expected frequency: 40-50% of employees.
Needs Improvement (NI): Not consistently meeting role expectations. A performance improvement plan (PIP) will be initiated within 30 days.
Unsatisfactory (U): Significant performance gap requiring immediate action.

SELF-ASSESSMENT GUIDELINES
Self-assessments are due two weeks before the manager assessment deadline. Employees should address: key accomplishments from the review period (with measurable outcomes where possible), areas for growth and development, goals for the next period, and feedback on team and manager effectiveness. Self-assessments should be 400-800 words. Bullet points are acceptable.

PEER FEEDBACK
Each employee selects 3-5 peers for feedback; managers may add up to 2 additional reviewers. Peer feedback is anonymous to the employee but visible to the manager. Peers are asked to rate collaboration, communication, technical contribution, and reliability on a 1-5 scale plus qualitative comments. Feedback givers are encouraged to be specific and cite concrete examples.

COMPENSATION REVIEW
Compensation changes are effective January 1 and July 1. Employees rated Exceptional receive merit increases of 8-12% plus equity refresh. Strong performers receive 4-7% increases plus equity refresh eligibility. Meets Expectations performers receive 2-4% cost-of-living adjustments. Employees on a PIP are not eligible for increases during the PIP period. Market adjustments (separate from merit) are conducted annually in Q4 and may apply to any employee whose compensation falls below the 25th percentile of market data.

PROMOTION CRITERIA
Promotions are evaluated twice per year, aligned with the review cycle. To be considered for promotion, an employee must have been in their current level for at least 18 months, received at least one Strong or Exceptional rating, and have demonstrated sustained performance at the next level for at least one full quarter. Promotions are calibrated across the company by a cross-functional committee. An employee may be recommended for promotion by their manager and must be endorsed by the department VP.

DEVELOPMENT PLANS
Every employee, regardless of rating, should have a documented development plan after each review cycle. Development plans include: 2-3 skill areas to develop, specific learning resources or experiences, milestones to track progress, and a timeline. Managers are responsible for actively supporting development plans through project assignments, mentorship, and learning opportunities.
