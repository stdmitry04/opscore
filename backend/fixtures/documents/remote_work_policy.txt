REMOTE WORK POLICY — Acme Corporation
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

ELIGIBILITY
All full-time employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible for remote work arrangements, subject to manager approval. Part-time employees working more than 20 hours per week are also eligible after 90 days. Contractors and temporary workers are not covered by this policy.

CORE HOURS
Remote employees must be available and responsive during core hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local timezone, Monday through Friday. Outside core hours, employees have flexibility to structure their workday as needed to meet deliverables. Meetings should not be scheduled before 9:00 AM or after 5:00 PM local time without prior consent.

EQUIPMENT AND STIPENDS
The company provides a company-issued laptop to all eligible remote employees. A one-time home office stipend of $750 is available to employees who have been remote for more than 6 months, subject to manager and finance approval. The stipend covers ergonomic equipment, monitors, keyboards, and desk accessories. Internet and phone costs are the employee's responsibility and are not reimbursed. Employees may expense up to $50/month for a co-working space if they lack a suitable home office environment.

SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
All remote work must be performed on company-issued devices or personal devices enrolled in the company Mobile Device Management (MDM) system. Use of public WiFi networks requires an active VPN connection at all times. Employees must not access company systems from jurisdictions on the export control list. Screens must be locked when stepping away from the workstation. Video calls involving confidential information should not be taken in public places.

PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS
Remote employees are evaluated on outcomes and deliverables, not hours worked. Managers must set clear quarterly OKRs with remote direct reports. Response time expectations: Slack messages during core hours — within 30 minutes; emails — within 4 business hours. Remote employees are expected to attend all-hands meetings and team retrospectives, either in person or virtually. Quarterly in-person attendance at the main office is encouraged and travel costs are reimbursed.

EXCEPTIONS AND APPROVALS
Requests for fully remote arrangements (no office days required) must be approved by the employee's VP and HR. Hybrid arrangements (1-3 days in office per week) require only direct manager approval. Employees in their first 6 months are encouraged to be in office at least 3 days per week for onboarding purposes.
